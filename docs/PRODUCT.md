# TTG Ops: product page

Written Nov 15 2026 from Roni's own requests (Oct 8 to Nov 7). This page outranks the code. Where it is silent, ask Roni.

## The job
Help Roni, as Clinical Director, stay on top of every Together They Grow case: who serves whom and when, what the clinical notes say,
whether incoming billing matches the record, and what needs his decision. It is an oversight tool. It sends nothing.

Who uses it: Roni only. One owner key; BCBAs, providers and staff never sign in or see anything. "Students by BCBA" is only a way for Roni to sort his own list.

## Defining moment
Roni on his phone between other work, wanting the one answer: "Is anything slipping, and what do I need to decide?"

## Home: exactly six portals
1. Students & Cases: one complete student view. Child info, Cases, Providers, Schedule, Billing, Documents, Clinical Data. Empty fields hidden.
2. Clinical Notes: browse and search notes across students, providers and dates.
3. Providers & Schedules: who serves whom, the week's calendar, coverage gaps, assignments. Provider page: Provider info, Case roster & schedule, Billing sheets, Documents, Clinical data. SSN last 4 only.
4. Billing Review: incoming billing against the record and notes, flags, his sign-off. No invoices, no payments.
5. Reports & Updates: drafts built from clinical notes, for his review.
6. Email & Open Items: drafts and follow-ups. Approving marks a draft for Katya; nothing sends from the app.
Plus: search, and "Needs your review" in separate sections (messages, reports, billing, gaps, schedule problems).

## Non-goals (permanent until Roni reopens them)
- No seventh portal. No import or data-coverage portal on Home.
- No sending email or texts. No billing decisions beyond his own recorded sign-off. No invoices or payments.
- No guessing identities. A note or line that cannot be matched to exactly one record stays apart.
- No unexplained colors or dots. Words first.
- No real data in the public repo, the preview, or screenshots.
- No access for anyone but Roni. No BCBA, provider or staff logins.
- No case numbers on screen. They stay in the source data for the billing staff who submit to the state.
